# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Automation Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Agni-glitch, through GitHub. If the matter is
sensitive, ask in an issue for a private way to get in touch, without including details. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that break this Code of Conduct. Depending on the severity, they may give a warning, a temporary
ban, or a permanent ban from the project.
